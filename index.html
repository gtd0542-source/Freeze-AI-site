Frequently asked questions

Everything you
want to know.

Another question? Write to us at contact@frezzai.app.

Will I have to buy ingredients?

Usually not: Frezz AI starts from what you already have and aims for zero extra shopping. If an idea still needs one more ingredient, the app tells you before you start, and you can pick another idea.

Is Frezz AI free?

The fridge photo and food detection are free. To see the dinners found and cook them step by step (exact quantities, guided steps, other ideas), you need Frezz Premium: a 3-day free trial with no credit card, then $9 for 1 month or $59 for 1 year, as a one-time payment. Nothing renews automatically.

Are my photos stored?

No. Your photo is only used to spot your food: it is analyzed by Google Gemini (paid service, not used to train its models), then it is stored neither by us nor in your account.

Are my allergies and diet respected?

Your restrictions are checked before and after the AI’s suggestion, by automatically detecting forbidden ingredients. This check does not replace reading labels: always check them, especially for severe allergies.

Why do I need an account?

Your account keeps your household, restrictions and dinners, and brings them back on all your phones. Sign in with one tap using Google or your email.

Where is my data stored?

Your account, preferences and dinners are hosted in the European Union (Ireland). Photos are analyzed by Google, and this website is served by Vercel, in the United States: everything is detailed in the privacy policy. Your data is never sold, and you can delete everything from the app at any time.
