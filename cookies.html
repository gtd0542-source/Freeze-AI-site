Cookies and trackers

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Our approach

A tracker is a file or a piece of information stored in your browser (cookie, local storage, fingerprint). On frezzai.app, only Whop’s advertising pixel sets one for a purpose other than running the website: it only loads after your consent.

Visit statistics (Vercel Web Analytics) and speed measurements (Speed Insights) are anonymous, cookie-free, and do not need your consent.

Your choice, and how to change it

On your first visit, a banner offers "Reject all", "Accept all" or "Customize my choices". Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and nothing advertising-related loads until you accept. Your choice is kept for 6 months, then asked again.

You can change your mind at any time: (also at the bottom of every page). Withdrawing your consent deletes Whop’s cookies and storage from your browser.

You can also delete cookies in your browser settings, or block trackers with an extension: the website works without them.
