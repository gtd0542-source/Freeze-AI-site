Cookies and trackers

Everything is detailed on the Cookies page. You can change your mind at any time: .

Your photos

Your fridge photos are only used to identify food. They are sent to the Frezz AI server, then to Google Gemini for analysis, and are never stored, neither by us nor in your account.

Sign in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", we only receive your email address, your name and your profile photo (openid, email and profile scopes). This information is only used to create your account and sign you in. We do not access any other data in your Google account (Gmail, Drive, contacts…).

Frezz AI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This data is never sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Your rights

You can access your data, correct it, delete it, ask for a copy to take elsewhere (portability), object to processing based on legitimate interest, ask for it to be restricted, and withdraw your consent at any time (allergies, news, advertising pixel). Residents of France can also leave instructions about their data after their death.

  • In the app: Settings → Delete all my data immediately deletes your account and all its content.
  • On this website: .
  • For everything else: contact@frezzai.app. We reply within one month.

If you live in the European Union, you can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Security and children

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS) and each account can only read its own data. Frezz AI is not intended for children under 15.
